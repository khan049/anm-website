A school built to bring bright students back into the mainstream

Many capable children fall behind because they lack good teachers and a good environment. An-Nisa Mission exists to change that: a calm residential campus, qualified faculty and a routine that builds both knowledge and character.

Since 2010 the Mission has been supported by the G.D. Charitable Society, which funded the land and school building and awards merit scholarships to deserving students.

Our campus

Classrooms, hostels, a prayer space and open grounds, all within one campus in Chakmakarampur.

What every day here includes

📚

Group coaching

Experienced teachers run coaching inside the Mission, backed by weekly, fortnightly, monthly, half-yearly and annual exams.

🩺

Health care

Resident students get regular check-ups, and a team of doctors looks after anyone who falls ill.

🏃

Morning PT

A trained instructor leads physical training every morning, with games each afternoon and an annual sports competition.

🕌

Faith and values

Congregational prayers, Quran recitation and respect for every religion shape a well-rounded character.

💬

Counselling and feedback

Students and teachers meet for counselling twice a month. Parents receive feedback reports on each class.

🧭

Beyond the classroom

Quizzes, seminars, debates, a school magazine, an annual educational tour and national day celebrations.

Admissions

Students join Classes 5 to 9 through an entrance test, or directly through the M-CAT. Class 11 admissions are also open. The admission form costs ₹150 (Classes 5–9) or ₹200 (Class 11).

  1. Get the formCollect the admission form from the Mission office or enquire by phone.
  2. Sit the testTake the 2 hour 30 minute written test, or qualify through the M-CAT.
  3. Submit documentsBring the documents listed below, with photocopies and photographs.
  4. Confirm admissionPay the admission charges and January month fee at the time of admission.

Entrance test

Written test of 2 hours 30 minutes, 100 marks. Questions come from the syllabus of the class below the one you are applying for.

Scholarships and concessions. In 2024–26, 30% of needy and meritorious students receive a half or one-quarter fee concession.

Documents to bring

  • Photocopy of birth certificate and original school leaving certificate (online T.C.)
  • Photocopy of the latest marksheet
  • Original annual income certificate of parent or guardian
  • Two passport-size and two stamp-size colour photos of the student, and photos of the guardians
  • Aadhaar card copies for the student and both parents or guardians
  • Bank passbook copy, and OBC certificate if applicable
Class-wise entrance syllabus
  • Class 5: Class 4 textbooks for Bengali, English and Science. Mathematics from Nabo Ganit Mukul.
  • Class 6: Class 5 syllabus, including English comprehension from an unseen passage.
  • Class 7: Class 6 syllabus in all subjects, plus short paragraph writing.
  • Class 8: Class 7 syllabus: arithmetic, algebra, geometry, physical and life science.
  • Class 9: Class 8 syllabus, plus voice and narration and joining sentences.

Uniform and house rules

Uniform
  • Boys: white-green check shirt with green trousers (Classes 5–8); white shirt with black trousers (9–10); white shirt with blue trousers (11–12). Navy blue sweater and black shoes in winter. White panjabi, pyjama and cap for prayer.
  • Girls: white-green check kameez with green salwar and orna (5–8); white kameez with maroon salwar and orna (9–10); white kameez with navy salwar and orna (11–12 Science); white with green orna (11–12 Arts).
  • Lungis and jeans are not permitted.
Key rules
  • Follow the daily routine, prayer times and the Mission's code of conduct.
  • No leaving the campus without permission. Parents may visit on Sundays and special holidays with an identity card.
  • Mobile phones, iPads, radios and musical instruments are not allowed and are confiscated if found.
  • Students must be back on the day holidays end, otherwise a late fine of ₹250 applies.
  • Students who repeatedly break discipline may be asked to leave.
Why families choose us
  • Syllabus follows WBBSE and WBCHSE.
  • Respect for all religions, with Islamic education alongside.
  • Special support and fee concessions for meritorious and low-income students.
  • Prizes for strong annual results and good conduct.
